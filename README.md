There is a canvas section to the right of your screen and sidebar to the left of the screen.

<img width="1198" height="778" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/890c90fc-62f4-4e02-b2a3-ddff70ab5ac9" />

The sidebar serves as a tool to add devices to the canvas. Each device has a drop-down menu with a list of options.

<img width="1197" height="778" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a988e7a4-2054-4b98-8248-4966da0d0028" />
<img width="1198" height="779" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/315b7c28-c627-4ba5-81d9-19afa22e1e98" />

In the drop-down menu you'll have a list of options to control how the devices interact with each other.
  - Choosing which device to connect to.
  - Delete device
  - Option to turn device on or off.

<img width="1197" height="778" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6468c649-9173-496b-aaec-55ad331308d8" />

After you've chose your device connections, you'll need to choose a port for them to share information through.

<img width="1197" height="778" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/911ed6d6-1c7c-497c-bbc2-4eb875da620c" />
<img width="1198" height="777" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d3417a83-58fa-4cb4-b920-91c3992a1053" />

Now that your network topology is set-up, you can click "Animate" on the upper right of your screen to begin a simple packet simulation.

<img width="1197" height="777" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1408a05f-b0c5-4820-84a7-07121f91c1aa" />
